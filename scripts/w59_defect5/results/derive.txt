## 1 The core word `sqWord s x y`
  sqWord.a : 0 terms beyond the Frobenius head -4x+2y, degree 0
  sqWord.b : 0 terms beyond the Frobenius head -4x+2y, degree 0
  sqWord.c : 0 terms beyond the Frobenius head -4x+2y, degree 0
  sqWord.d : 0 terms beyond the Frobenius head -4x+2y, degree 0
  sqWord.e : 0 terms beyond the Frobenius head -4x+2y, degree 0
  sqWord.f : 5 terms beyond the Frobenius head -4x+2y, degree 2
  sqWord.g : 5 terms beyond the Frobenius head -4x+2y, degree 2
  sqWord.h : 5 terms beyond the Frobenius head -4x+2y, degree 2
  sqWord.i : 5 terms beyond the Frobenius head -4x+2y, degree 2
  sqWord.j : 23 terms beyond the Frobenius head -4x+2y, degree 3
  sqWord.k : 23 terms beyond the Frobenius head -4x+2y, degree 3
  sqWord.l : 23 terms beyond the Frobenius head -4x+2y, degree 3
  sqWord.m : 79 terms beyond the Frobenius head -4x+2y, degree 4
  sqWord.n : 79 terms beyond the Frobenius head -4x+2y, degree 4
  sqWord.o : 248 terms beyond the Frobenius head -4x+2y, degree 5

### the five abelian rows are exactly the relator vector
  [PASS] sqWord.a = -4 x.a + 2 y.a
  [PASS] sqWord.b = -4 x.b + 2 y.b
  [PASS] sqWord.c = -4 x.c + 2 y.c
  [PASS] sqWord.d = -4 x.d + 2 y.d
  [PASS] sqWord.e = -4 x.e + 2 y.e

### the top coordinate does not read any slot's own top coordinate
  [PASS] sqU6Core has no s.o, x.o, y.o
  [PASS] sqU6Core4 has no s.m, x.m, y.m
  NOTE the class-five core reads the slots' coordinates up to class FOUR, the
  sigma slot's s.m and s.n included (conjugation moves the top row by
  g.n * p.a - g.m * p.e).  That is the exact analogue of the class-three core,
  which reads the class-TWO coordinates s.d, s.e and no class-three one: at every
  class the core reads everything strictly below the top row and nothing at it.
  [PASS] sqU6Core reads s.n
  [PASS] sqU6Core reads s.m

### which lower coordinates the class-five core reads
  variables in sqU6Core : s_a, s_b, s_c, s_d, s_e, s_f, s_g, s_h, s_i, s_j, s_k, s_l, s_m, s_n, x_a, x_b, x_c, x_d, x_e, x_f, x_g, x_h, x_i, x_j, x_k, x_l, x_m, x_n, y_a, y_b, y_c, y_d, y_e, y_f, y_g, y_h, y_i, y_j, y_k, y_l, y_m, y_n

  [PASS] sqWord.f matches the committed SqU6.sqWord_f
  [PASS] sqWord.j matches the committed sqU4Core through toU4LL

### sizes of the two genuinely new core rows
  sqU6Core4 (class four, the (1,5) row): 79 terms, degree 4
  sqU6Core  (class five, the (1,6) row): 248 terms, degree 5

## 2 Products of gamma_2 elements: what is additive and what is not
  (p*q).a : additive
  (p*q).b : additive
  (p*q).c : additive
  (p*q).d : additive
  (p*q).e : additive
  (p*q).f : additive
  (p*q).g : additive
  (p*q).h : additive
  (p*q).i : additive
  (p*q).j : additive
  (p*q).k : additive
  (p*q).l : additive
  (p*q).m : CROSS p_f*q_h
  (p*q).n : CROSS p_g*q_i
  (p*q).o : CROSS p_f*q_l + p_j*q_i

## 3 The handle block at class five
  the handle commutators are gamma_2, so the additive coordinates are plain
  sums and the class-four and class-five coordinates carry a STRICTLY
  TRIANGULAR cross-handle pairing (vanishing at h <= 1).
  [PASS] handle block closed form at h = 0
  [PASS] handle block closed form at h = 1
  [PASS] handle block closed form at h = 2
  [PASS] handle block closed form at h = 3
  [PASS] handle block closed form at h = 4

## 4 The assembled class-five defect
  (sqRelWord m).o = -4 (m 1).o + 2 (m 2).o + sqU6Defect h m,
  sqU6Defect h m = sqU6Core (m 0) (m 1) (m 2)
                 + W.a * H.n + W.f * H.l + W.j * H.i + H.o
  with W = sqWord (m 0) (m 1) (m 2) and H the handle block.
  [PASS] assembled defect at h = 0 (generic marking)
  [PASS] assembled defect at h = 1 (generic marking)
  [PASS] assembled defect at h = 2 (generic marking)
  [PASS] assembled defect at h = 3 (generic marking)

## 5 The class-four row, for the same reason
  (sqRelWord m).m = -4 (m 1).m + 2 (m 2).m + sqU6Defect4 h m,
  sqU6Defect4 h m = sqU6Core4 (m 0) (m 1) (m 2) + W.a * H.k + W.f * H.h + H.m
  [PASS] assembled class-four defect at h = 0 (generic marking)
  [PASS] assembled class-four defect at h = 1 (generic marking)
  [PASS] assembled class-four defect at h = 2 (generic marking)
  [PASS] assembled class-four defect at h = 3 (generic marking)
