  class-five transport is the committed `conFourShift` with the class-three data
  (h, i) replaced by the class-four data (m, n) and the outermost abelian column

  `sqRelWord_conFourDress_j` carries: the relator's own a-row against the
  dressing's Frobenius class-four n-row.  Nothing new in shape appears at class
  five; what was missing was the carrier and the defect, not the shape.

  the cross term the committed theorem carries; so the two agree exactly.
