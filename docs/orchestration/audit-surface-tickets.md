## Ticket briefs

**V0 (opus, read-only, main checkout).**  Input: `docs/axiom-closure.md` (the 24-module,
115-definition union).  For each module in the union, classify its contents: (a) vocabulary
definitions in the closure, (b) theorems/constructions that are *not* in any axiom's
statement closure.  Measure what a clean cut would achieve: proposed target layout
`GQ2/Foundations/Vocab/*.lean` (importing only Mathlib and each other), which existing files
become pure re-exporters, and which defs have bodies entangled with proof-layer constructions
(the obstructions — e.g. `DyadicOrientation` reaching `D₀` through `DyadicPresentation`, B10's
`OrientedTameQuotient` reaching `GammaA` through `BoundaryFrame`).  Deliverable:
`docs/orchestration/audit-v0-recon.md` with a per-module cut list and a predicted final
import-closure count for `Foundations/Axioms.lean`.  No edits.

**V1 (fable).**  Turn V0 into a migration design: file-by-file move plan (names and
namespaces unchanged — moves only, re-export `public import` shims at the old locations so no
consumer changes), the import DAG of the new subtree, and the V2 ticket cut.  Owner gate
before V2 dispatch: approve the layout and the target metric (proposal: `Foundations/Axioms`
transitive project closure ≤ 15 files, every file in it importing only Mathlib + the
subtree).  Constraint: module-system files only (`module` + `public import`); the paper-tag
ledgers are regenerated by paperforge after moves, not hand-edited.

**V2a–… (opus, mechanical).**  Execute V1's moves one target file per ticket, disjoint
owned-file sets, `lake build` after each.  A move that turns out to be entangled gets
reported back to the board rather than forced.

**V3 (opus).**  Point `GQ2/Foundations/Axioms.lean`'s imports at the Vocab subtree, drop the
now-unneeded wide imports, regenerate `docs/axiom-closure.md` and both Compass reports
(`docs/atlas.md` procedure), and record the achieved closure number in this board and in the
Zulip thread reply.

**S0 (fable, design).**  Design the B9 statement bundling: a documented
"quadratic Kummer setting" structure (`k`, `d`, `δ`, `β`, `a` + the side conditions
`hδ`/`hdeg`/`hβ`/`hβ0`) and an "index-2 open stabilizer datum" structure (the
`hidx`/`s`/`hs`/`htriv`/`hUo` block that `corH1`/`evensNormH2`/`⌣` all thread), with
per-field docstrings citing the corresponding clause of Kahn Th. 2.  Decide and price the
two open questions for the owner gate: (i) drop `hdeg` (provable via
`finrank_quadExt_eq_two`; kept 2026-07-24 by the Q1 decision to mirror Kahn's `L/k` setting —
the Zulip preference for smaller statements weighs the other way; a `hdeg`-free form can also
keep a redundancy lemma adjacent as the faithfulness witness); (ii) split the `∧` into
separate degree-1 and degree-2 axioms (census 9 → 10; the reviewer explicitly prefers
more-but-simpler).  Also decide the churn model: restate the axiom in place and update its
consumers, vs. bundled axiom under a new name + old positional form re-derived as a same-name
theorem (the zero-churn B9-A pattern; requires the census-name updates in
`scripts/check_axioms.sh` `AUDIT_EXPECTED_AXIOMS`, `comparator-config.json`
`permitted_axioms`, `formalization.yaml`, and `GQ2/AxiomLedger.lean`).  Deliverable:
`docs/orchestration/audit-s0-design.md` + the gate questions.
