## 0. Route pivot, recorded up front

Option (b) as priced in the decision packet was "formalize the graded-Lie fragment
(Lazard embedding, Serre §7's own route), ~2–4k lines".  During design validation the
orchestrator read Serre 252 §6–7 in the original (numdam scan; §7 gives only "brèves
indications" at q = 2) and built a numerical harness that computes in the honest free
group via truncated Magnus expansion (`scratchpad/span_model.py`, results §5).  Two
findings changed the optimal route:

1. **The full Magnus/Lyndon counting architecture validates end-to-end** (filtration
   compatibility, PBW-family separation `rank = N_k`, column span, `coker(d̄) = 2`
   patched exactly by the tails — k ≤ 6, both roles).  It would work, at the priced cost.
2. **The L4b termination obstruction dissolves under a different factoring.**  L4b's
   (SQ2) loop treated `π(d̄-atom)` factor-by-factor.  Taken as a *whole word*,
   `π(d̄_{k-1}(w)) = d̄_k(w²)` — π of a level-(k−1) defect atom **is** a level-k defect
   atom at the squared modification (machine-validated, §5).  And `[v, t_twisted]` is
   *definitionally* `(v²)⁻¹·d̄(v,1,1)`.  With those two observations, **option (a)'s
   strong induction on k closes with no termination measure at all** (§2): the only
   recursion is `S(k−1) ⇒ S(k)` itself.

So the campaign's main lane is the induction proof (§2), which is what the graded-Lie
viewpoint *reveals* but which formalizes in the repo's existing λ-calculus with no new
algebra.  The Lazard machinery proper (Magnus coefficient algebra) is built anyway as a
**parallel insurance lane** (§4, GL-D): it is the fallback certificate route for the
base case, it is the tool that would rescue the step if a hidden flaw surfaces, and its
coefficient functionals are exactly the `ker(ab)`-sensitive functionals that the open
SL1 design gap will eventually need (decision packet §2A — not this campaign's scope,
but the infrastructure is shared).

**Why this is still "the (b) decision":** the deliverable is unchanged — `span_free_r0/r2`
proved with no axiom, census frozen at 9, opus workers, no fable-tier proof search with
unknown termination.  The step and base are fully specified below; nothing is left to
worker-side proof invention beyond routine λ-calculus of the kind L2/L4c already did.

## 1. The statement being discharged

`StageLemma.lean`: for `k ≥ 3`, in `Q_{k+1} = levelQuot freeProTwo (k+1)`:

    zLayer k ≤ ⟨ d̄-image over modifications w ∈ (λ_{k-1}-image)³  ∪  two tails ⟩

- r0-shape: `d̄(w) = w₀²·[w₀,a]·[w₁,y]·[w₂,s]` at the marked generators
  `(a,s,y) = (freeGen 0, freeGen 1, freeGen 2)`; tails `s^{2^{k-1}}, y^{2^{k-1}}`
  (generators 1, 2).
- r2-shape: `d̄(w) = w₂²·[w₂,y]·[w₀,x]·[w₁,s]` at `(s,x,y) = (freeGen 0,1,2)`;
  tails `s^{2^{k-1}}, x^{2^{k-1}}` (generators 0, 1).

Uniform description: one generator is **twisted** (τ; appears only inside the coupled
slot `v²·[v,τ]`), the other two (β₁, β₂) carry the plain bracket slots and the tails.
r0: τ = generator 0; r2: τ = generator 2.  The two proofs are verbatim mirrors; no
permutation-automorphism machinery is used, the two shapes are just proved side by side
(house twin-lemma style).

Write `S(k)` for the statement, `W_k` for the right-hand closure, `V_k := zLayer k`.

## 2. The proof (main lane)

### 2.1 Step: `S(k−1) ⇒ S(k)` for `k ≥ 4`

All in `Q_{k+1}`; `G = freeProTwo`; instances and cited lemmas exist in-tree.

1. **Atomize.**  `lambdaImage_induction` (j = k−1): `V_k` is generated by
   sq-atoms `mk(v²)` and bracket-atoms `mk⁅v,g⁆`, `v ∈ λ_{k-1}`, `g ∈ G`.
2. **Bracket atoms are free.**  In class form, with `v ∈ lambdaImage (k−1) (k+1)` and
   `z ∈ Q_{k+1}`:
   - `commP v ·` is multiplicative and inversive in `z` (values central in `Z_k`), so
     `z` reduces along `Q_{k+1} = ⟨generator classes⟩` to `z ∈ {ā, s̄, ȳ}`.
   - `commP v β_i = d̄(w)` at the triple with `v` in the β_i-slot and `1` elsewhere —
     **an element of `W_k` by definition** (`dbarWord*_one`-style computation).
   - `commP v τ = (v²)⁻¹ · d̄(v,1,1)` — again definitional; `(v²)` is a sq-atom.
   So bracket atoms lie in `W_k · ⟨sq-atoms⟩`.
3. **Sq-atoms via `S(k−1)`.**  `v ∈ lambdaImage (k−1) (k+1)` projects along
   `levelProj k` into `zLayer (k−1) ⊆ Q_k`, which `S(k−1)` writes as a product of
   d̄_{k-1}-atoms and (k−1)-tails.  Pull the factorization back up: `v = (∏ fᵢ') · ζ`
   with `fᵢ'` lifts of the factors inside `lambdaImage (k−1) (k+1)` (λ-images are
   levelProj-surjective) and `ζ ∈ zLayer k` (= ker levelProj).  Then
   `v² = (∏ fᵢ')²` (ζ is a central involution) `= ∏ (fᵢ')²` (pairwise commutators in
   `[λ_{k-1}, λ_{k-1}] ⊆ λ_{2k-2} = 1` for k ≥ 3).  Per factor:
   - tail lift: `(s^{2^{k-2}}·ζ')² = s^{2^{k-1}}` = the level-k tail ∈ `W_k`;
   - d̄-atom lift: `(d̄-word(W)·ζ')² = d̄-word(W)²` and **the transport identity**
     `d̄-word(W)² = d̄-word(fun i => (W i)²)`  (§2.3) with `(W i)² ∈ lambdaImage (k−1)`
     — a level-k d̄-atom ∈ `W_k`.
   So sq-atoms ⊆ `W_k`, and with 1–2, `V_k ⊆ W_k`.  ∎

### 2.2 Base: `S(3)` (in `Q₄`; |Q₄| = 2^23 — structural, NOT decide)

Follows L4b's by-hand closure (their report; "colπ-at-π-towers, tails, left-normed
columns").  Skeleton of the argument, same atomization:

1. Bracket atoms: exactly as step 2 above (works for all k ≥ 3).
2. Sq-atoms `mk(v²)`, `v ∈ λ₂`: expand `v` by `lambdaImage_induction` at j = 1
   (`λ₂` generated by `u²` and `⁅u,g⁆`, `u` arbitrary).  Squaring is multiplicative on
   λ₂-classes mod λ₄ (`[λ₂,λ₂] ⊆ λ₄`), so two atom families remain:
   - `(⁅u,ζ⁆)²`: use `⁅u²,ζ⁆ = ⁅u,ζ⁆·⁅⁅u,ζ⁆,u⁆·⁅u,ζ⁆` (exact identity):
     `(⁅u,ζ⁆)² = ⁅u²,ζ⁆ · (central rearrangement) · ⁅⁅u,ζ⁆,u⁆⁻¹`; both right-hand
     factors are **bracket atoms at level 3** (`u², ⁅u,ζ⁆ ∈ λ₂`) — free by 1.
   - `u⁴` (u arbitrary): induction on a generator-word representative of `u mod λ₂`
     (λ₂-shifts die: `((u·c)²)² = (u²·c²·⁅c,u⁆)²` and each correction lands in
     bracket/λ₄-territory).  For `u = x·m`: `u⁴ = x⁴·m⁴·(products of level-3
     bracket-atom classes and ⁅m,x⁆-squares — the previous family)`.  Generator
     4th powers: `β_i⁴` = the two k=3 tails; `τ⁴ = d̄₃(τ²,1,1)` since `⁅τ²,τ⁆ = 1`
     (the diagonal trick).
3. Assemble.

The base is the largest single fill and the one with residual formalization friction
(the u⁴ word-length induction).  Fallback if it snags: the GL-D Magnus functionals
give a finite certificate route at k = 3 (14 explicit spanning elements, coordinate
separation); escalate to orchestrator before self-inventing a third route.

### 2.3 The transport identity (GL-A's core)

For `k ≥ 4`, generic pro-2 `G`, slots `a s y : Q_{k+1}` arbitrary, and
`w : Fin 3 → Q_{k+1}` with `w i ∈ lambdaImage (k−2) (k+1)`:

    dbarWordR0 a s y w ^ 2 = dbarWordR0 a s y (fun i => w i ^ 2)     (r2 mirror)

Proof calculus (all in-tree styles; L4c's shift-formula file is the model):
- the four factors of `d̄(w)` lie in `lambdaImage (k−1) (k+1)`
  (`w i ∈ λ_{k-2}` ⇒ `w i ² ∈ λ_{k-1}`, `⁅w i, g⁆ ∈ λ_{k-1}`);
- squares of products of `λ_{k-1}`-image elements are products of squares
  (`[λ_{k-1}, λ_{k-1}] ⊆ λ_{2k-2} = 1` in `Q_{k+1}` for k ≥ 3);
- `(commP u g)² = commP (u²) g` for `u ∈ λ_{k-2}`: the correction
  `⁅⁅u,g⁆,u⁆ ∈ [λ_{k-1}, λ_{k-2}] ⊆ λ_{2k-3} = 1` needs **k ≥ 4** — this is the only
  place the step threshold enters, and at k = 4 it is exactly tight (λ₅ = 1 in Q₅).

Validated numerically at k = 4, 5, 6, both shapes, 6 random structured modification
triples each (`scratchpad/step_check.py`, §5).
