There are two separable points in what you wrote.

**"Stated with only the definitions in Mathlib."**  I agree that's the right north star, and
one axiom already meets it: B1 (topological finite generation of `G_ℚ₂`) is Mathlib's
`Field.absoluteGaloisGroup` plus one one-line `abbrev`.  For the other eight it is genuinely
impossible today rather than a choice: the statements need continuous cochain cohomology of
profinite groups *with cup products* (Mathlib's `ContCohomology` framework was redefined
2026-07-02 and is still moving; cup products so far exist only in FLT's staging tree), local
reciprocity/class formations (nothing upstream), free profinite groups and profinite
presentations (nothing), and Stiefel–Whitney classes of quadratic forms (nothing).  Using the
abstract `groupCohomology` instead would be a faithfulness bug, not a simplification — for
profinite `G` with finite coefficients, abstract and continuous cohomology genuinely diverge
at `H²`.  We went through this in detail in an earlier review round
(`docs/angdinata-review-plan.md` §2 has the capability table); the plan is to converge on the
upstream vocabulary as it stabilizes, not to maintain a rival one.

**"The imports make it hard to audit; the B9 statement is impenetrable."**  This part was
actionable, and most of it is now in the repo:

* `Challenge.lean` no longer imports the axiom file at all.  Its imports are statement
  vocabulary only (`AbsGalQ2` arrives through `GQ2.GammaA`'s public imports — the axiom
  import was only ever routing that name).  The nine axioms belong to the permitted
  *solution* trust base in `comparator-config.json`, not to the challenge statements.
* `GQ2/Foundations/Axioms.lean` now contains the nine `axiom` declarations and **nothing
  else**.  The same-name derived theorems that used to sit next to them (the
  formerly-axioms we've since proved: the dyadic Hilbert-symbol formula, unramified
  unit-norms, Kummer surjectivity, the unit filtration, and the derived `evensKahn_dyadic`)
  moved to `GQ2/Foundations/Interfaces.lean`.  Their *proofs* were what dragged the proof
  layer into the axiom file's imports.
* New: [`docs/axiom-closure.md`](docs/axiom-closure.md), regenerated by
  `scripts/axiom_closure.sh`.  For each axiom it lists the exact set of project constants a
  reader must understand to know *what the axiom asserts* — the transitive closure of the
  statement through definition bodies and structure fields, with proofs pruned (a theorem
  reached by a definition contributes only its machine-checked statement).  The numbers: B1
  needs 1 project definition, B5 needs 12, B7 needs 13 (all of them the continuous-cochain
  `H⁰/H¹/H²` layer), and B9 — the one you quoted — needs 37.  The union across all nine is
  **115 definitions + 12 referenced theorem statements**, each linked to source with its
  docstring.  That's the real audit surface; the import graph overstates it.

On B9 specifically: fair hit, and it's the axiom we're restructuring next.  Two things worth
knowing meanwhile.  The hypothesis pile is deliberate: `hdeg` is provable from the other
hypotheses and is carried so the setting is *literally* Kahn's `L/k` setting (Invent. Math.
78 (1984), Théorème 2), and the `(α, hαdef, hα, hαc)` block pins `α` to the Kummer cocycle of
the chosen square root — extra hypotheses make an axiom weaker, hence safer to assert.  And
the docstring directly above it explains every binder against the Kahn/Evens/Kozlowski
sources (with `docs/literature-axioms.md` §B9 as the ledger).  The planned change
(`docs/orchestration/audit-surface-tickets.md`): bundle the setting into two documented
structures — the quadratic-Kummer data and the index-2 open-stabilizer datum that
`corH1`/`evensNormH2`/`⌣` all thread — so the axiom reads as a few lines with named,
docstringed fields; and likely split the degree-1/degree-2 conjunction into two axioms, since
you're right that more-but-individually-checkable beats fewer-but-dense.
