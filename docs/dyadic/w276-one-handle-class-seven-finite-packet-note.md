# W276 — the finite class-seven compact packet

W275 proved that the ordinary one-handle class-seven obstruction depends only on the four
compact correction factors modulo `lambda_7`.  W276 packages exactly those attained quadruples
as a finite type and descends both the quotient obstruction and the literal row equation to it.

The resulting `compactPacketObstructionValues` is a finite subset of the ordinary relative
cokernel.  Its identity-membership test is equivalent to the existence of a legal relative lift
satisfying the ordinary row equation.  Hence full W6-to-W7 correctability implies that the
identity occurs in this finite value set.

This is an exact finite reduction, not a proof that the identity occurs.  Nor is identity
membership alone sufficient for the full correction: the character-neutral `C` condition is a
separate obstruction and does not descend under the weaker modulo-`lambda_7` equivalence.
