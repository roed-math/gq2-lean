# W141: isolated ramified Npc power charge

The truncated omega2 charge is now identified and polarized exactly.

- On an odd fixed-point-free action coboundary, `NpcJet.powCharge` equals the charge obtained by
  conjugating the action line through a slice.
- Its polar is an explicit sum of quadratic polar terms and a factor-set term.
- Bilinearity of the chosen factor set would make the isolated charge quadratic.
- A proved gauge-change construction can alter it by an arbitrary normalized function, showing
  that isolated quadraticity does not follow from the abstract equivariant factor-set axioms.

The generic ramified proof must therefore cancel the section-dependent factor-set term against
the other terms in the full W140 diagonal.  The highlighted declarations use exactly the
standard three axioms.
