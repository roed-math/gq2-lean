# W229: the parity obstruction has three scalar characters

W228 reduces the one-handle neutrality interaction to filling the parity plane
`(ZMod 2)^2`. W229 dualizes that finite target. Its three nonzero characters are the first
coordinate, the second coordinate, and their sum. A subgroup fills the plane exactly when
all three character images are nontrivial.

Applied to the balanced interaction row, this gives two exact formulations. Success is
equivalent to three scalar CABD relations, one detected by each character. Failure is
equivalent to one of those characters annihilating every admissible CABD relation. The
uniform three-character supply is equivalent to the parity supply from W228, so together
with ordinary-relative coordinate coverage it implies `MpcPivShiftH 2 1`.

No scalar CABD relation is asserted here. The neutrality front is now the explicit task of
defeating three mod-two annihilators. This is the right interface for the graded relator and
cup-pairing machinery: a failed span has become a concrete functional, not an opaque proper
subgroup.
