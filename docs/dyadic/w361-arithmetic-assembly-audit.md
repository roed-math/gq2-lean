## 1. What the inherited bundles actually assert

`LocalReciprocity.norm_reciprocity` includes surjectivity and the norm-kernel
identity at each finite abelian Galois layer **over Q₂**. The new chain uses
this existing field of the supplied bundle. It does not derive full local
class field theory from unrelated weaker assumptions.

`MarkedRecip B` supplies the marked reciprocity map, density, norm
compatibility with base reciprocity, the two-primary unramified coordinate
and its unit/uniformizer clauses, the cyclotomic-level data, and its existing
`K(i)` bridge. It does not contain full relative finite-layer norm
reciprocity. The new proof genuinely avoids adding that missing statement.

`OrientedTameQuotientK T` contains a closed normal pro-two subgroup, an actual
quotient equivalence with `Tq (qOf K FF)`, and two-primary unit/uniformizer
orientation. These are substantive inherited inputs, not empty predicates.
The quotient equivalence supplies surjectivity of `T.tameFK`.

“Standard-three” below refers to the kernel dependency of a theorem
**parametrized by these bundles**. It does not mean the existence of those
bundles has been proved from logic. Instantiating the globally supplied
bundles additionally invokes their existing axioms `localReciprocity`,
`markedRecipAt`, and `orientedTameQuotientAt`.

## 2. Inertia equality, not signed normalization

The forward route has these concrete steps:

1. Odd-root-generated finite Q₂ layers have ramification index one, by
   injectivity of reduction on odd roots and the proved degree formula.
2. Base finite-layer norm reciprocity makes their reciprocity unit image
   trivial. Odd-root separation therefore puts reciprocity units in actual
   residue inertia.
3. Marked norm compatibility transports that unit statement to arbitrary K.
   Density makes every unit-trivial two-adic character vanish on the marked
   unramified kernel, without identifying its uniformizer scalar.
4. The wild subgroup is pro-two, so its full residue image vanishes once its
   two-primary image vanishes. The descended residue character kills tame
   inertia because the tame relation makes that image torsion and Zhat is
   torsion-free.

The reverse route uses the **full** unramified tame coordinate, not just its
two-primary projection. Its continuous Zhat endomorphism has an image
containing a nonzero integer: a positive power of an absolute residue
Frobenius lift fixes the finite base field. The proved endomorphism criterion
then gives injectivity and equality of inertia kernels.

Thus `fullTameInertia_comap_eq_absoluteInertiaK` proves the required subgroup
equality. It does not assert a chosen Frobenius sign, scalar one, or an
equality of fully normalized characters. No such stronger assertion is
silently used downstream. In particular the proof does not conflate full
inertia with the kernel of the two-primary unramified character.

## 3. Relative ramification and actual norm witnesses

`RamifiedCertificateInertiaFixedField` identifies the field fixed by actual
relative inertia with `K ⊔ Ω`, where Ω is generated by all odd roots of unity.
Finite generation then places each individual fixed element in
`K ⊔ Q₂(ζ)` for one primitive odd root ζ. This is a proved containment, not
an assumed unramifiedness predicate.

`RamifiedCertificateRelativeOddCyclotomic` constructs the actual action
`Gal(L/K) → Gal(kL/kK)`. The residue action is faithful for an odd-root
generator. Comparing the field-degree and residue-degree towers forces
`e(L/K)=1`; no normality of K/Q₂ is required. Its concrete cyclotomic wrapper
proves relative Galoisness and generation, rather than exposing them as
unproved premises. Its final equal-norm theorem constructs the upper
filtration internally and returns a nonzero element of K with the same norm.

The anchored-field containment uses the correct direction: actual inertia
maps into full tame inertia. Taking fixed fields reverses inclusion and
places the anchored tame-inertia field in `K ⊔ Ω`. This yields the prescribed
relative `e=1` packet and actual norm partners, not merely oddness of an
unrelated quotient index.

`AnchoredInvolutionRelativeNorm` removes the old absolute odd-degree
restriction. The finite tame leg has odd relative ramification, the anchored
base leg is unramified, and relative ramification multiplies in towers.
For the involution field k, `e(L/k)` divides both two and the odd `e(L/K)`, so
it equals one. The proof establishes `K ≤ k`, which is necessary for this
cancellation. Arbitrary absolute ramification of K is allowed.

The returned `HasEqualNormValueGroups` is the repository's literal spectral
norm convention. The proof constructs the square-root generator and uses
every represented element, with nonzeroness retained. It does not substitute
an unrelated notion of unramifiedness without proving the norm clause.
