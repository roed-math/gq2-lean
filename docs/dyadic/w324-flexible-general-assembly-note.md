therefore separates the genuinely general assembly from `SelectedPresentation`.

marked-core realization.  `GenAsmFlexibleRowResidualRN` asks only for the existing corrected

interface.  Thus the four already-closed family lanes and all frozen regressions remain intact;
the new surface only removes the unjustified demand that every future solution be one of the
