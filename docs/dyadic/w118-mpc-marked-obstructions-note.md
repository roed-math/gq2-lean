# W118: five necessary marked-successor obstructions

The marked successor now carries the recursive invariant that its concrete moves actually
preserve.  The strengthened state `MpcMkNuGood` records a good witness whose head correction is
neutral under `nuM`.  The class-five base has this property, and every full marked advance
preserves it.

For such a witness, the explicit secondary defect lies in five canonical kernels:

1. the crossed-derivation offsets at `A`, `C`, and `D`;
2. the top additive digit detected by `nuM`;
3. the one-deeper cyclotomic digit detected by `chiM`.

These are necessary vanishings only.  The module does not identify their common kernel with the
marked cost range and does not assert successor propagation.  The remaining uniform theorem is
therefore a reflection statement for this specific five-kernel fiber, not generic surjectivity.
