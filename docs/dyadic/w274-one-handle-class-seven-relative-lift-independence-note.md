# W274 — class-seven relative lift independence

The class-seven construction first chooses a compact successor packet and then chooses direct
relative lifts of its four core factors.  W273's literal row equation appeared to depend on both
choices.  This wave removes the second dependence.

For two direct lifts of the same compact packet, their coordinatewise ratios are exact relative
depth-five classes.  The `C` ratio is character-neutral because both lifted `C` factors satisfy
the neutral row condition.  The four ratios therefore form a legal standard relative correction,
and the new residual is the old residual multiplied by its standard relative shift.

It follows that the two inverse targets represent the same class modulo the standard coordinate
span.  Ordinary relaxed coverage, the literal row equation, and the zero-or-one augmented rank
jump are consequently independent of the arbitrary direct lift choices.  The remaining finite
question depends only on the selected compact class-seven successor packet.

This wave does not compare different compact successor packets, nor does it solve the row equation
or the separate character-neutral `C` lifting condition.
