# W287: the neutral class-seven connecting homomorphism

W286 expressed full W6 correctability as a finite connecting fiber followed by a neutral-C
filter. W287 identifies the algebra behind that second filter.

The kernel of `oneHandleCompactConnectingHom` acts on each nonempty ordinary connecting fiber.
Its relative shift lies in the relaxed coordinate span and therefore defines
`oneHandleCompactNeutralConnectingHom`, with target the finite neutral-C cokernel. The neutral
obstruction transforms by the inverse of this homomorphism.

Consequently, after fixing any ordinary correction, full W6 correctability is equivalent to
membership of its neutral obstruction in `compactNeutralConnectingValues`. The remaining
one-handle class-seven problem is thus a second finite homomorphism-image test, ready for the
same transpose/rank treatment as the ordinary connecting obstruction. No membership claim is
made in this wave.
