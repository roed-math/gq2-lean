# W315: a realized M certificate already contains the pivot

W315 combines the W314 marking-orbit theorem with the field realization carried by corrected
assembly.  Transporting the realization along the certificate's target automorphism produces
the canonical marked-core realization.  This works for compact M and for every positive-level
Mpc display and handle count.

Consequently, field orientation rigidity turns any realized corrected M certificate into the
exact distinguished-C pivot.  The equivalent characteristic-core formulation needs only one
oriented field equivalence.  For compact M, a strict W313 handle marking is incompatible with
any realized corrected certificate.

This removes the apparent analytic detour.  A new certificate at the handle marking would not
avoid the general pivot problem; together with its field realization it would already solve
that problem.  The remaining even-family work is therefore the intrinsic orientation/pivot
boundary, not a separate family of handle-phase or nilpotency-class calculations.
