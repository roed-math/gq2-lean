# W226: the neutrality cokernel is a quotient of `(ZMod 4)^2`

W225 gave every ordinary relative `C` class a unique pair of deep handle digits modulo
exact row-neutral corrections. W226 transports the residual `C`-versus-`ABD` interaction
through those coordinates.

The resulting coordinate factor is surjective onto the neutrality cokernel, and its kernel
is exactly the range of the literal interaction row. Consequently the neutrality cokernel
is a quotient of `Multiplicative (ZMod 4) × Multiplicative (ZMod 4)` and its order divides
sixteen. For any chosen representative of a target obstruction, vanishing is equivalent to
membership of its digit pair in that interaction range.

This is a strict finite reduction, not a proof that the interaction range is all of the
coordinate group. The remaining mathematical task is to compute which of the sixteen digit
pairs are realized by the central-layer `C = A * B * D` equation.
