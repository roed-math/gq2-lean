# W239: three scalar parity checks

W239 dualizes the canonical parity obstruction from W238. The parity plane has exactly three
nonzero characters: the two coordinate characters and their sum.

Whenever one of these characters kills every interaction row, its value on the selected
target is independent of the chosen mod-four lift. The canonical parity obstruction is
nontrivial exactly when one such character is nontrivial on the target. Equivalently, parity
coverage is the conjunction of three explicit scalar compatibility implications.

The parity part of the balanced one-handle front is therefore finite and Boolean. After
these three checks, the only possible coordinate failure is one of the six primitive
order-four directions.
