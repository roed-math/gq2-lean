# W325: flexible certificate supply

W324 removed the frozen five-row table from the final corrected assembly.  W325 removes the
same restriction from certificate supply.

`FlexibleCoreLeavesRN` contains an arbitrary `CorePresentation` and the normalized sigma and
wild `Ztwo` rows.  The generic core bridge derives the candidate-to-core map, its exact maximal
pro-2 kernel, surjectivity, and cyclotomic compatibility.  No selected branch or canonical word
appears in this interface.

`FlexibleCertificateLeavesRN` therefore retains only five genuinely word-dependent inputs:
tame specialization, exact lifting, Stokes duality, the scalar certificate, and the affine
determinant.  `wordCertificateRN_of_flexibleLeaves` fills every routine field of
`WordCertificateRN`, and the two final handoffs feed those leaves directly through W324 to the
compatible general-K assembly.

All structural declarations and the certificate assembler have exactly the standard three
axioms.  The end-to-end field theorem inherits only the established Hilbert, Stiefel-Whitney,
Tate-duality, finite-generation, and Euler-characteristic inputs from the general assembly.

This makes the remaining general M problem precise.  W323 rules out the unchanged canonical
compact-M presentation in the odd-residue `M0 2` pattern.  It is now enough to construct a
tame-compatible Nielsen transform whose word and core presentation are adapted to the mobile
pivot, and transport the five non-routine leaves across the resulting source equivalence.  No
new selector or assembly theorem is needed.
