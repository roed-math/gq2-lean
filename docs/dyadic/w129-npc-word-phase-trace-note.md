# W129: scalar word trace for arbitrary-unit Npc

At scalar coefficients, the resolved procyclic-N word family has the tau coordinate in both
Heisenberg rows.  The generic word-cohomology trace construction therefore gives a canonical
additive equivalence from the displayed `WordH2` group to `ZMod 2` at the uniform resolver.

This is the blocker-independent scalar seam needed before identifying `QZero` with a graph
Hessian.  It does not claim that identification and does not supply the still-missing unramified
full-offset Hessian formula.  Both exported declarations use exactly the standard three axioms.
