## 0.  Headline

**`SqClassClearingSupply h 4` is NOT discharged, and it is NOT refuted.**  Two things changed.

1. **Wave 69's route is located.**  `cfour_supply_of_eichDefect` asks `cfourEichDefect nu' j = 1`.
   That element projects onto wave 68's class-three defect (`cfourId_eichDefect_levelProj`), so
   the hypothesis **forces `bridgeDefect nu' j = 1`**, and then the *undressed* frame already
   solves the class-three problem (`cfourId_base_relator_three_of_eichDefect`).  Wave 68's entire
   argument is the construction of a dressing to cancel exactly that defect.  So the trivial
   dressing is being asked to do a job wave 68 already had to hire a dressing for.  This is not a
   refutation: it is a measurement of how strong the hypothesis is, and it says the route is the
   wrong one rather than a false one.

   **Nothing committed is thereby wrong.**  `cfour_supply_of_eichDefect` is a true, proved
   implication; this bears on the strength of its antecedent, not on its truth.  Wave 68's
   `sqClassThree_supply` is true and proved, and never claimed its dressing was minimal.  And
   since `bridgeDefect nu' j ≠ 1` is proved nowhere in the tree, it does not even follow that
   wave 68's dressing *is* necessary.  The claim is conditional in both directions: at any
   marking where wave 69's antecedent holds, wave 68's dressing was avoidable there.  So proving
   that antecedent entails proving wave 68's central construction was unnecessary, which is
   evidence the route is mis-aimed, not proof that it is closed.

2. **The obligation moved from `Y` to `Z`.**  Dress with wave 68's own tuple and correct inside
   `γ₂`.  Wave 68's `bridge_relator_mem` already says the resulting relator dies to class three,
   so the class-four leftover lies in `Z = γ₃/γ₄` (`cfourId_leftover_mem_zLayer`), an elementary
   abelian 2-group in which the leftover is its own inverse.  A `γ₂`-correction is free on both
   rows and free on mod-2 independence.  Hence
   ⭐⭐⭐ **`cfourId_supply_of_reach`**: class four follows as soon as some `γ₂`-valued, exactly
   row-trivial correction of wave 68's frame kills the relator in `Q₄`.

   Against wave 69 that is one full graded layer down (`Y = γ₂/γ₄` to `Z = γ₃/γ₄`) and a subgroup
   to hit instead of a point.  `CfourIdReachable h` names the remainder;
   `cfourId_markTransitivity_of_reachable` carries it plus the classes `k ≥ 5` all the way to
   `SqLamMarkTransitivity h`.

## 1.  The named next step: `γ₃/γ₄` on the tower (module `GradedGammaThreeFour`)

The tree had this only on slices.  Three things now exist at the tower level, for an arbitrary
topological group.

* **The two homomorphisms out of `Y`.**  Wave 69 had bimultiplicativity *against* a `Y`-slot; the
  computation actually needs the `Y`-slot *inside* the bracket, and it holds for the same reason
  (`gThreeFour_ad_left`, `gThreeFour_ad_right`, plus both inverse laws).  And
  `gThreeFour_commP_sq_left`: `commP (p²) q = (commP p q)² · commP (commP p q) p`, the group form
  of `ad (x^{[2]}) = (ad x)²`.  Note this is the exact complement of `cfour_commP_sq_left`, where
  the *far* slot is a `Y`-element and the whole expression dies.
* **⭐⭐ Jacobi** (`gThreeFour_jacobi`):
  `commP (commP p q) r * commP (commP q r) p * commP (commP r p) q = 1` in `Q₄`.  The tree had no
  Jacobi and no Hall-Witt identity anywhere; the subgroup-level three-subgroups lemma
  (`commutator_commutator_le_of_rotate`) was the only relative, and it cannot move one element.
  The route is the free-group Hall-Witt identity (`gThreeFour_hallWitt`) plus two `Q₄`-reductions:
  a triple bracket is central, and an inverse in the middle slot is invisible.

  **This is standing tooling, not a local step.**  `gThreeFour_hallWitt` is an identity in an
  arbitrary group, closed by `group`: no topology, no level, no class.  And `gThreeFour_jacobi_of`
  isolates the passage from Hall-Witt to Jacobi as an implication over an arbitrary group, taking
  the two reductions as its hypotheses.  A run at any other layer supplies its own two facts and
  gets Jacobi without touching the word; `gThreeFour_jacobi` is merely the `γ₃/γ₄` instance, and
  it is the only class-four-specific declaration in that section.
* **⭐⭐ Generation** (`gThreeFour_zLayer_le`): a **closed** subgroup of `Q₄` holding `y²` for every
  `y ∈ Y` and `commP y q` for every `y ∈ Y`, `q ∈ Q₄` holds all of `Z`.  This is the tower form of
  `γ₃/γ₄ = V^{[4]} ⊕ (Λ²V)^{[2]} ⊕ L₃(V)` in the "generated by" direction, which is the direction
  a witness search consumes.  `cfourId_zLayer_le` discharges the closedness hypothesis at
  `D_sq h`, where the tower is discrete.

## 3.  Wave 69's `x₁`-bundle analysis: VERIFIED, with the justification CORRECTED

Wave 69 recorded, as analysis, that the delicate direction is `⁅⁅x₁, g⁆, x₁⁆`, that Jacobi does
not reach it, and that it is instead reached "by a correction whose square part involves `x₁`",
admissible because "the `x₁`-coordinate functional is never in the span of `λ` and `ν'` mod 2".

**The mechanism is right and is now a theorem.  The justification is not the one wave 69 gave.**

* Jacobi indeed degenerates in that direction: in characteristic two the rotation of
  `⁅⁅a, x₁⁆, x₁⁆` returns the same term twice and says nothing.
* The correction is `c = x₁²`, and `cfourId_x1_sq_direction` computes its knob against a slot `q`
  as `(commP x₁ q)² · commP (commP x₁ q) x₁` (i.e. `ad(x₁)²`), which is exactly that direction.
* Admissibility needs no span argument and never mentions the `x₁`-coordinate functional.  Both
  rows of `x₁²` lie in `4ℤ₂` **outright**: `λ(x₁²) = 4` since `λ(x₁) = 2` (`nuLam_x1`), and
  `ν'(x₁²) = 0` since `ν'(x₁) = 2ν'(x₀)` for *every* `ν'` (`toAdd_nu_dsqX1`) and `ν'(x₀) = 0` at a
  selected marking.  Rows in `4ℤ₂` are repairable **inside `γ₃`** by wave 68's `bridge_row_image`
  at `m = 2`, and a `γ₃`-repair moves neither the bracket part of a knob
  (`cfourId_comm_zLayer_dress`) nor its square part (`cfourId_sq_zLayer_dress`).
  `cfourId_row_repair` and `cfourId_x1_sq_admissible` are the two steps.

The span argument wave 69 offered is about `V`; the functionals that actually constrain a knob
live on `W = γ₂/γ₃` and are `λ mod 4`, `ν' mod 4`.  They vanish on the bracket part of `W` and
read `λ mod 2`, `ν' mod 2` on the square part.  That is a different, and easier, condition.

## 4.  ANALYSIS, NOT COMMITTED: where the remaining obligation sits

> **WITHDRAWN BY WAVE 71.  Read `docs/dyadic/w71-cfour-note.md` §0 and §7 before using anything
> below.**  (Forward pointer added by W72-HOUSE, watch round 9; the wave-71 note itemises the
> corrections but this note was never marked.)  In summary: the fourth-power directions
> `σ^{[4]}`, `x₀^{[4]}` identified below are **not directions the leftover has**
> (`cfourKnob_leftover_eq` shows its only fourth power is `A⁴`); the `w⁴` cancellation invoked at
> the end of this section is **unnecessary**, since `A⁴` is the square knob of the admissible
> correction `A²`; and the bullet below that concludes `K ⊇ L₃(V)` **does not establish it**,
> because the knob it appeals to is that bracket times `(commP x₁ a)²`, by this wave's own
> `cfourId_x1_sq_direction`.  The headline sentence below ("the whole of class four is now one
> question ... If that survives contact with a proof, class four is true") therefore does not
> stand: what is actually left is the three factors of `w71-cfour-note.md` §4, of which the main
> one, the two class-four defects, is a factor this section did not flag as hard at all.  What
> this wave *committed* is unaffected: its Lean statements are untouched and wave 71 uses them.

None of this section is formalised.  It is recorded because it is the sharp form of the wave-71
ticket, and because it rests on tools this wave did commit.

Write `V = G/γ₂`, `W = γ₂/γ₃`, `Z = γ₃/γ₄`.  For the free pro-2 group the graded object of the
lower 2-central series is the free **restricted** Lie algebra on `V` with `deg(x^{[2]}) = deg x + 1`,
so `W = V^{[2]} ⊕ Λ²V` and `Z = L₃(V) ⊕ V^{[4]} ⊕ (Λ²V)^{[2]}` (wave 69's decomposition).

Let `W' ⊆ W` be the **admissible** classes, those with both rows in `4ℤ₂`; by §3 that is
`ker(λ mod 2) ∩ ker(ν' mod 2)` on the square part and all of `Λ²V` on the bracket part, so
codimension at most two in `W`.  The knob subgroup is

```text
  K = ⟨ [W', g] for g a frame letter other than x₁ ;  φ(c) = c^{[2]} + [c, x₁] for c ∈ W' ⟩ .
```

The frame letters other than the `x₁`-slot span exactly the `x₁`-free hyperplane of `V`: the
dressing `A ≡ u^{ν'(v)} v^{ν'(u)}` and the pivot `w ≡ σ + x₀` involve no `x₁`, so the change of
basis is unipotent on `span{σ, x₀, u_*, v_*}`.

* **`K ⊇ L₃(V)`.**  A bracket `⁅⁅a, b⁆, c⁆` with `c ≠ x₁` is a knob directly.  With `c = x₁` and
  `a, b ≠ x₁`, Jacobi rewrites it into two brackets whose outer slots are `a` and `b`, both
  `≠ x₁`.  The only surviving case is `⁅⁅a, x₁⁆, x₁⁆`, and that is `[x₁^{[2]}, a]`, the knob of
  §3 with `a ≠ x₁`.  So Jacobi plus the `x₁`-square exhaust `L₃(V)`.
* **The bundle then unbundles.**  For `c ∈ W'`, `[c, x₁] ∈ L₃(V) ⊆ K` (whether `c` is a bracket
  class or a square class, since `[g^{[2]}, x₁] = ad(g)²(x₁)`), so `φ(c) ∈ K` gives `c^{[2]} ∈ K`.
  Hence `K ⊇ L₃(V) ⊕ sq(W')`.
* **What `K` misses.**  `sq` is injective on `W`, so `Z/K` is a quotient of `W/W'` and has
  dimension at most two.  On the square part of `W` the two constraining functionals are
  `λ̄ = κ_σ + κ_{x₀}` and `ν̄' = κ_σ + Σ_j (ν'(u_j) κ_{u_j} + ν'(v_j) κ_{v_j})`, using
  `λ(σ) = sqPivotExp` odd, `λ(x₀) = 1`, `λ(x₁) = 2`, `λ(u) = λ(v) = 0` and, at a selected
  marking, `ν'(σ) = 1`, `ν'(x₀) = ν'(x₁) = 0`.  These are independent, so the codimension is
  exactly two and the missing part of `Z` sits in `V^{[4]}`, represented by `σ^{[4]}` and
  `x₀^{[4]}`: the fourth powers of the two generators whose square class fails admissibility.
  It is not a vacuous space:
  `GQ2.ContCoh.dsqSigma_pow_four_not_mem_twoCentralSeries_four` (`SqCore/TwoCentralFacts.lean`)
  says `σ⁴ ∉ γ₄`, so `σ^{[4]} ≠ 0`.

**So the whole of class four is now one question: does the leftover have a component in the
fourth-power directions of the two odd-row generators?**  Wave 69's other analysis point answers
it in the negative: its `A⁴` term contributes `(u⁴)^{ν'(v)} (v⁴)^{ν'(u)}`, and writing
`u² = c·w^{2ν'(u)}`, `v² = d·w^{2ν'(v)}` the two handle letters each contribute one copy of `w⁴`
to the power `ν'(u)ν'(v)`, which cancel because `w⁴` has order two.  If that survives contact
with a proof, **class four is true**, and the witness is wave 68's dressing corrected by an
explicit `γ₂`-valued, exactly row-trivial tuple.  Nothing negative is claimed anywhere.

The wave-71 ticket is therefore sharp: **evaluate the leftover `cfourIdLeftover nu' j` in `Z`,
and exhibit it as a product of knob values** (equivalently, discharge `CfourIdReachable h`).  The
two hard sub-steps are the `L₃(V)` spanning claim above, which needs the *relations* half of the
graded decomposition and not only `gThreeFour_zLayer_le`'s generation half, and the fourth-power
cancellation.

## 4a.  Relation to the sibling lane `GradedRelatorBase.lean` (W70-FIVE)

That lane generalised wave 69's `cfour_relator_base` to every class and moved it upstairs:
`relBase_relator_base` says `sqRelWord (sqArbBase h nu' j) = conjP (relBaseEichDefect nu' j) t`
in `D_sq h` itself, unconditionally, and `relBase_relator_base_mem_iff` turns it into an `iff`
inside any normal subgroup.  `relBase_supply_of_eichDefect k` is then the all-`k` form of
`cfour_supply_of_eichDefect`.

**The two lanes do not overlap and neither subsumes the other.**

* Their result is about the *trivial* dressing at every class; ours is about *wave 68's*
  dressing corrected inside `γ₂` at class four.  These are two different sufficient conditions
  for the same target `SqClassClearingSupply h 4`, and either alone would discharge it.  Our
  `cfourId_supply_of_reach` does not use `relBase_supply_of_eichDefect` and is not weakened by
  it.
* Their §4 docstring already records that their clause is only *sufficient* for the supply and
  that "wave 68's class-three witness is a nontrivial dressing, so the gap is real".  §0 above is
  the quantitative form of exactly that remark: `relBase_defectDies_iff_trivial` says their
  clause **is** "the trivial dressing clears class `k`", and `cfourId_eichDefect_levelProj` says
  that at `k = 4` it forces `bridgeDefect nu' j = 1`, i.e. forces wave 68's dressing to have been
  unnecessary at that marking.  So the gap they name in prose is here measured against a
  committed object.
* Their `relBase_eichDefect_four` (`levelMk _ 4 (relBaseEichDefect nu' j) = cfourEichDefect nu' j`)
  composes with `cfourId_eichDefect_levelProj` to give the defect at three consecutive levels:
  upstairs in `D_sq h`, in `Q₄` as wave 69's `cfourEichDefect`, and in `Q₃` as wave 68's
  `bridgeDefect`.  A later wave that wants `cfourId_bridgeDefect_of_eichDefect` derived from the
  schema rather than by hand can get it from `relBase_relator_base_levelMk` at `k = 3` and
  `k = 4` plus antitonicity; the content our statement adds, and which their file does not
  contain, is the identification of the level-three *value* with `bridgeDefect` itself
  (`cfourId_base_relator_three`).  Their `k = 3` instance is an `iff` about triviality, not an
  evaluation.
