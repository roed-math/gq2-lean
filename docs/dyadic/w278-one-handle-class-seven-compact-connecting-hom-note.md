# W278 — the compact-kernel connecting homomorphism

W277 placed all attained compact packets in one affine raw-cost fiber.  W278 constructs the
corresponding connecting homomorphism explicitly.  A direct level-six correction whose compact
cost vanishes has a shift in the one-handle relative layer; quotienting by the four relaxed
ordinary coordinate rows gives

`oneHandleCompactConnectingHom : OneHandleCompactKernelCorrection →*
  OneHandleC7StandardRelativeCokernel`.

For any two legal relative lifts, their direct correction ratio belongs to the compact-cost
kernel.  The obstruction of the second lift is the obstruction of the first multiplied by the
inverse connecting image of this ratio.  Thus the packet obstruction is a genuine torsor
cocycle controlled by one named kernel map, rather than an opaque dependence on chosen lifts.

If this connecting homomorphism is trivial, the finite obstruction-value set from W276 is a
singleton and the literal ordinary row equation is lift-independent across all legal packets.
The wave does not assert triviality, identity of that singleton, or the separate neutral-C
condition.  The next exact question is whether the compact raw-cost kernel maps into the relaxed
relative coordinate span.
