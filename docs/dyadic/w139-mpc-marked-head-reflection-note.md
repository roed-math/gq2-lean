# W139: uniform M6 head reflection

The corrected marked-source exactness problem is now reduced to one all-degree theorem.

- The unrestricted, head-marked, and fully marked source maps and their cost/obstruction maps are
  formalized, with base-row independence.
- Vanishing of `nu` and `deltaD` constructively moves the square-bearing head slot into the
  marked `B^2` carrier modulo the next filtration rung.
- The M-frame proof handles torsion, parity, deep C/D corrections, injective coordinates, and
  density rounding uniformly for every depth at least two.

The sole remaining statement is head completion: restricting the other three slots from the
unrestricted depth subgroup to their marked carriers does not shrink the cost image.  Exact
finite calculations verify it in depths 2, 3, and 4, but the all-degree theorem remains open and
is used only as an explicit conditional input.  All exported declarations are standard-three.
