# W305: the canonical marked-core realization boundary

W305 weakens the remaining all-degree M-family premise to the object the corrected assembly
actually consumes.  The compact and procyclic word certificates do not require the field
equivalence to preserve the canonical orientation, nor do they inspect which source letter
carries the minimal unramified value.  They require a `MarkedCoreRealization` at the canonical
M marking.

The two exact predicates are:

- `M0CanonicalMarkedCoreRealization B alpha h`;
- `MpcCanonicalMarkedCoreRealization B alpha r h epsilon eta`.

Given either predicate, W305 constructs the corresponding `GenAsmBranch` directly and attaches
the already-unconditional arbitrary-h compact-M or arbitrary-display Mpc corrected word
certificate.  No oriented equivalence or distinguished-C pivot occurs in these consumer
theorems.

`EvenCanonicalRealizationBoundary` retains these predicates only on M0 and epsilon-false Mpc.
N0, Npc, epsilon-true Mpc, and odd L remain closed.  The headline
`hasSelectedRowResidualRN_of_fieldSelectionFromRange_of_realizationBoundary` is therefore the
new sharp all-degree dispatcher.

W304 still matters: it proves that every selected M field has an exact-level value somewhere
in `ker chiM`.  But W305 shows that moving that value to the historical `C` coordinate is only
one sufficient construction of the needed realization.  A general full-marking normalization,
or an alternate canonical M normal form, can now close the campaign without proving the
stronger oriented-C statement.
