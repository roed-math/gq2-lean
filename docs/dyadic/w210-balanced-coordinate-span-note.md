# W210: the four constrained coordinate spans

W210 decomposes the W209 relative finite shift into its four literal coordinate ranges.
The finite source is the product of three copies of the relative depth image, in coordinates
`A`, `B`, and `D`, and the character-neutral relative depth image in coordinate `C`.
`oneHandleBalancedCoreLevelCorrection_eq_coords` proves that these four inclusions generate
the entire source.

After composing each inclusion with `oneHandleBalancedCoreRelativeShiftHom`, the subgroup
`oneHandleBalancedCoreCoordinateSpan` is the supremum of their four ranges. The theorem
`oneHandleBalancedCoreRelativeShift_range_eq_coordinateSpan` proves exact equality with the
full W209 range. This is a direct-product decomposition, not a new surjectivity assumption.

The four value formulas identify the generators precisely:

- the `A` row is `p^2 * [p,A] * [p,B]`;
- the `B` row is `[p,A]`;
- the neutral `C` row is `[p,D]`;
- the `D` row is `[p,C]`.

Here `p` ranges over the relative image in the first, second, and fourth rows, and over the
character-neutral relative image in the third. Thus the remaining one-handle Mpc question is
now exact and finite: prove that the current relative relator target lies in the subgroup
generated by these four constrained row families. The broader augmented raw span theorem
does not by itself establish this restricted statement.

`OneHandleBalancedCoreCoordinateSpanSupply` is definitionally the target-specific version of
that boundary. It is equivalent to W209's relative range supply and therefore implies
`MpcPivShiftH 2 1` through `mpcPivShiftH_two_one_of_balancedCoordinateSpan`.

and `Quot.sound`.
