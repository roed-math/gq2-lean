# W316: the general M orientation theorem is a finite naturality statement

W316 removes ambiguity from the remaining orientation front.  The complete presentation
predicate `IsPresentedOrientationM` is already known to characterize `chiM` from the four
Labute generator values and value one on every handle.  Its pullback naturality under all core
automorphisms is equivalent to `MCoreOrientationCharacteristic`.

On the field side, `MFieldCyclotomicRowsAt` asks for exactly those same finite values after
pulling the cyclotomic character back along every abstract equivalence from `DM alpha h` to
`G_K(2)`.  For `alpha >= 1`, this is equivalent to full `MFieldOrientationRigidityAt`.  Once
one oriented field equivalence is fixed, the core and field finite-row formulations are
equivalent.

The two final consumers feed this finite boundary directly into the realized-certificate
orbit argument for compact M and for arbitrary-display, arbitrary-handle Mpc.  Therefore the
next general theorem is not a new nilpotency-class calculation or a new handle normal form.  It
is the intrinsic naturality theorem saying that the dualizing/cyclotomic orientation satisfies
these finite pullback rows.  Existing mod-two cup and Bockstein data cannot prove it because
they do not distinguish a character from its inverse; an integral dualizing-orientation or an
equivalent higher-character calculation is still required.
