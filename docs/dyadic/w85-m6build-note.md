## 2.  The class-four defect, in closed form (§1, §2 of the module)

Read in `Q₄ = G/λ₄`, for an arbitrary topological group, with `Y = λ₂/λ₄` and the central
involutive layer `Z = λ₃/λ₄`:

⭐⭐ `mpcBld_mWord_four`:

    mWord 2 (p·z·σ) (q·τ) (q²·r·μ) (w·ν)
      = mWord 2 p q r w · (σ²·⁅σ,p⁆ · ⁅p,τ⁆·⁅σ,q⁆ · ⁅q,w⁆²·⁅⁅q,w⁆,q⁆·⁅r,ν⁆·⁅μ,w⁆)

for any four `Y`-corrections and any `z ∈ Z` (the ansatz uses `z = (q⁴)⁻¹`, i.e. the letter's
`B̄⁻⁴` factor).  Four ingredient lemmas, one per factor of `mWord`:

* `mpcBld_sq_letA`: `(p·z·σ)² = p²·(σ²·⁅σ,p⁆)`;
* `mpcBld_comm_letAB`: `⁅p·z·σ, q·τ⁆ = ⁅p,q⁆·(⁅p,τ⁆·⁅σ,q⁆)`;
* `mpcBld_pow_letC`: `(q²·r·μ)⁴ = r⁴`, the fourth power is blind to the whole shift;
* `mpcBld_comm_letCD`: `⁅q²·r·μ, w·ν⁆ = ⁅r,w⁆·(⁅q,w⁆²·⁅⁅q,w⁆,q⁆·⁅r,ν⁆·⁅μ,w⁆)`.

The two terms `⁅q,w⁆²·⁅⁅q,w⁆,q⁆` are the `B̄²` prefix's own cost, the Hall-Petrescu expansion of
`⁅q²,w⁆`, and they are the whole class-four obstruction of the bare ansatz.  This reproduces wave
84 §4.2's hand formula term for term, now elaborated.  Transported downstairs by
`mpcBld_defect_four` and killed by `mpcOrb_relation`.

## 3.  The class-four rung (§3)

⭐⭐ `mpcBld_mem_four_witness`: at `s = t = ⁅D̄, B̄⁆ = commP D̄ B̄` and `m = n = 1` the defect is

    ⁅D̄,B̄⁆²·⁅⁅D̄,B̄⁆,Ā⁆·⁅Ā,⁅D̄,B̄⁆⁆·⁅⁅D̄,B̄⁆,B̄⁆·⁅B̄,D̄⁆²·⁅⁅B̄,D̄⁆,B̄⁆

and all six factors lie in `Z`, which is central of exponent two, so the three cancellations are
the elementary abelian ones: `⁅D̄,B̄⁆² = ⁅B̄,D̄⁆²`, the two `Ā`-brackets are mutually inverse hence
equal, and `⁅⁅D̄,B̄⁆,B̄⁆ = ⁅⁅B̄,D̄⁆,B̄⁆` because the bracket's near slot inverts on `Y`
(`mpcBld_commP_inv_left`).  Hence `mpcBld_nonempty_four` and

⭐ `mpcBld_shift_of_forall_class_five`: `MpcPivShift 2` follows from class-`k` solvability for
`k ≥ 5` alone.

The `√−10` scripts of wave 84 were throwaway `𝔽₂` linear algebra in the Magnus model; nothing of
that kind is used here.  The formalization is the group-theoretic computation, and it agrees.

## 4.  The increment engine, and the residue as one linear question (§5)

Wave 84 §6's structural remark (each rung is a *single* linear condition, the Fox ideal being the
χ-twisted augmentation kernel) is here in the form a builder can use.

⭐⭐ `mpcBld_incr_t`: for `u ∈ λ_j` and **any** `s ∈ λ₂` and any `t, m, n` whatsoever,

    mWord 2 (letA s) (letB (t·u)) (letC m) (letD n) ≡ mWord 2 (letA s) (letB t) (letC m) (letD n) · ⁅Ā, u⁆   (mod λ_{j+2}).

⭐⭐ `mpcBld_incr_n`: the same for the `D̄`-slot, buying `⁅C̄₀, u⁆`.

Both are exact, class-uniform, and independent of the other three corrections; the general form
lives in `mpcBld_incr_far` / `mpcBld_incr_near` for an arbitrary group.  Because the target layer
`λ_{j+1}/λ_{j+2}` is elementary abelian and both maps are homomorphisms, the reachable increments
form an `𝔽₂`-subspace, and

⭐⭐⭐ `mpcBld_shift_of_bracket_supply`: if at every class `j ≥ 3` the defect of a class-`(j+1)`
solution can be cleared by one `Ā`-bracket and one `C̄₀`-bracket of χ-trivial `λ_j`-elements, then
`MpcPivShift 2` holds.

That is the residue with the guesswork removed.  **Note what it is not**: it is a *sufficient*
condition using two of the four slots, so a failure of the hypothesis does not refute `M6`; the
`Ā`- and `C̄₀`-slots contribute too (`σ²·⁅σ,Ā⁆·⁅σ,B̄⁆` and `⁅μ,D̄⁆` at class four), and their
class-uniform increments are **not** in this module.
