## 2.  The class-four defect, in closed form (§1, §2 of the module)

Read in `Q₄ = G/λ₄`, for an arbitrary topological group, with `Y = λ₂/λ₄` and the central
involutive layer `Z = λ₃/λ₄`:

⭐⭐ `mpcBld_mWord_four`:

    mWord 2 (p·z·σ) (q·τ) (q²·r·μ) (w·ν)
      = mWord 2 p q r w · (σ²·⁅σ,p⁆ · ⁅p,τ⁆·⁅σ,q⁆ · ⁅q,w⁆²·⁅⁅q,w⁆,q⁆·⁅r,ν⁆·⁅μ,w⁆)

for any four `Y`-corrections and any `z ∈ Z` (the ansatz uses `z = (q⁴)⁻¹`, i.e. the letter's
`B̄⁻⁴` factor).  Four ingredient lemmas, one per factor of `mWord`:

* `mpcBld_sq_letA`: `(p·z·σ)² = p²·(σ²·⁅σ,p⁆)`;
* `mpcBld_comm_letAB`: `⁅p·z·σ, q·τ⁆ = ⁅p,q⁆·(⁅p,τ⁆·⁅σ,q⁆)`;
* `mpcBld_pow_letC`: `(q²·r·μ)⁴ = r⁴`, the fourth power is blind to the whole shift
  [W86-LADDER5: at class four only; the collapse does not lift (`mpcLad_pow_letC_five` has four
  central extras)];
* `mpcBld_comm_letCD`: `⁅q²·r·μ, w·ν⁆ = ⁅r,w⁆·(⁅q,w⁆²·⁅⁅q,w⁆,q⁆·⁅r,ν⁆·⁅μ,w⁆)`.

The two terms `⁅q,w⁆²·⁅⁅q,w⁆,q⁆` are the `B̄²` prefix's own cost, the Hall-Petrescu expansion of
`⁅q²,w⁆`, and they are the whole class-four obstruction of the bare ansatz.  This reproduces wave
84 §4.2's hand formula term for term, now elaborated.  Transported downstairs by
`mpcBld_defect_four` and killed by `mpcOrb_relation`.

(`mpcBld_commP_inv_left`).  Hence `mpcBld_nonempty_four` and

⭐ `mpcBld_shift_of_forall_class_five`: `MpcPivShift 2` follows from class-`k` solvability for
`k ≥ 5` alone.

## 4.  The increment engine, and the residue as one linear question (§5)

Wave 84 §6's structural remark (each rung is a *single* linear condition, the Fox ideal being the
χ-twisted augmentation kernel) is here in the form a builder can use.

⭐⭐ `mpcBld_incr_t`: for `u ∈ λ_j` and **any** `s ∈ λ₂` and any `t, m, n` whatsoever,

    mWord 2 (letA s) (letB (t·u)) (letC m) (letD n) ≡ mWord 2 (letA s) (letB t) (letC m) (letD n) · ⁅Ā, u⁆   (mod λ_{j+2}).

⭐⭐ `mpcBld_incr_n`: the same for the `D̄`-slot, buying `⁅C̄₀, u⁆`.

⭐⭐ `mpcBld_incr_s`: the `Ā`-slot buys `u²·⁅u, Ā⁆·⁅u, b⁆`, where `b` is the `B̄`-letter as it
stands.  This is the only slot that sees another letter, which is why it is stated against the
letter and not against a marked generator.

⭐⭐ `mpcBld_incr_m`: the pivot slot buys exactly `⁅u, d⁆`.  Worth recording separately: the
row-shifted fourth power `(q²·r·μ)⁴` does **not** see the increment at all, uniformly in the
class, so the pivot slot is as cheap above class four as it was at class four.
[W86-LADDER5: this sentence is about `mpcBld_incr_pivot` and STANDS at every class; it is the
*absolute* collapse `mpcBld_pow_letC` (§2's bullet) that fails to lift.  Two different claims.]

All four are exact, class-uniform, and stated in an arbitrary topological group first
(`mpcBld_incr_far`, `mpcBld_incr_near`, `mpcBld_incr_head`, `mpcBld_incr_pivot`).  Because the
target layer `λ_{j+1}/λ_{j+2}` is elementary abelian and the maps are homomorphisms, the reachable
increments form an `𝔽₂`-subspace: `mpcBldIncr t n us ut um un` is the whole effect of a
`λ_j`-move on the four corrections, and `mpcBld_step` turns a killed defect into the next rung's
solution explicitly.  Hence

⭐⭐⭐ `mpcBld_shift_of_bracket_supply`: if at every class `j ≥ 3` *any* defect in `λ_{j+1}` can be
cleared by one `Ā`-bracket and one `C̄₀`-bracket of χ-trivial `λ_j`-elements, then `MpcPivShift 2`
holds (two slots, hypothesis independent of the solution);

⭐⭐⭐ `mpcBld_shift_of_full_supply`: the same over all four slots, i.e. the **weakest one-step
supply the ansatz admits**.

That is the residue with the guesswork removed.  **Note what it is not.**  Both are *sufficient*
conditions.  The hypothesis of the full form quantifies over a class-`(j+1)` solution, and a
class-`(j+2)` solution need not be a `λ_j`-move away from one, so failing it does not refute
`M6`.  The refutation route is unchanged: by `mpcWord_shift_of_forall_class`, a **proven empty**
`mpcWordSet k` at one class would refute `M6` outright, and nothing weaker will do.
