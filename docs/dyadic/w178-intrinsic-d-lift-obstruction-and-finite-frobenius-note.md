# W178: intrinsic D, lift obstruction, and finite Frobenius

W178 sharpens all three live general-family fronts without adding a premise or an axiom.

## Intrinsic compact-M D placement

`M0FieldUniformizerDInvariant.lean` proves that the mod-two distinguished `D` character
transported to the field group is independent of the chosen oriented compact-M equivalence.
The ratio of two oriented equivalences preserves `chiM`, so the group-level invariance of
`dmDeltaDRed` identifies the characters.

Consequently `M0UniformizerDPlacement` is an intrinsic field condition.  Under this condition,
the compact-M pivot is exactly `M0UniformizerArtinCompatibility`, and the selected corrected-RN
consumer no longer exposes a frame choice.  The existence of the placement and the Artin value
remain the two honest arithmetic tasks.

## One-handle lift-choice obstruction

`MpcOneHandleDefectObstruction.lean` extracts the equation-only predicate
`MpcOneHandleDAbsorbable`.  Any solution forces a specific additive row equation.  Twisting the
`A` lift by the first standard handle preserves all four quotient lift equations, topological
generation, and the universal `C + 2B` row, but changes the necessary equation by exactly `2`.
Thus the original tuple and its twist cannot both admit a D-slot absorber.

In particular, lift equations, generation, and the universal row do not force defect
absorption.  A proof of the existential balanced lift must control a genuinely nonabelian
commutator equation; choosing arbitrary lifts and repairing them afterward is ruled out.

## Finite-residue Frobenius bridge

`RamifiedCertificateAbsoluteResidueFiniteFrobenius.lean` constructs the canonical algebra from
the base valuation ring to the absolute valuation ring, proves that the absolute residue field
is algebraic over the two-element base residue field by descent to finite simple subextensions,
and specializes Mathlib's finite-field theorem: on every finite residue extension the Galois
group is generated by powers of the square Frobenius.

The remaining absolute theorem is now isolated sharply: prove algebraic closedness of the
absolute residue field, the residue exact sequence and quotient topology, and the normalized
continuous equivalence of its absolute Galois group with `Zhat`.  Compatibility with the
oriented tame quotient is still the commuting Frobenius square recorded in W177.
