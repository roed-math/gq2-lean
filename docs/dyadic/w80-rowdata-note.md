## 2.  T-A1: the level, upper half, unconditional

`fieldUni_level_eq_zero_of_decomp` collapses the level from one `x : Kˣ` lying in `ker χ` with
`ν_ur(rec x)` a unit.  The same argument with `x` of `ν`-value `−2` instead of `−1` bounds the
level by `1`, and the witness is available at every ramified quadratic field at once:

| clause | why | tree |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `rec_K(2) ∈ ker χ_K` | `N_{K/ℚ₂}(2) = 2^{[K:ℚ₂]}`, unit part `1` on the nose | `fieldUni_norm_twoUnit` |
| `ν_ur(rec_K 2) = ofAdd(−2)` | `2 = (a/2)⁻¹ · (√a)²`, and `√a` is a uniformizer | `fieldUni_rootUnit_{lt_one,max}` |

so `nu_ker_chi_le` puts `−2 = (−1)·2^1` inside `2^{B.r} ℤ₂` and `mpcCert_le_of_two_pow_eq` gives
`B.r ≤ 1`.  Landed: `mpcRow_level_le_one_of_decomp` (general `K`),
`mpcRow_level_le_one_quadField` (every `ℚ₂(√a)` with `v(a)` odd and `‖a‖ = ‖2‖`), and the two
rows `mpcRow_level_le_one_sqrt{Ten,NegTen}`.  Standard three axioms.

The cofactor `(a/2)⁻¹` is never named: `mpcRow_norm_cofactor` reads `‖u‖ = 1` off the norm
identity `‖x‖ = ‖π‖ⁿ`, so the decomposition is `2 · ((√a)²)⁻¹` by construction and the arithmetic
is one line.

**What this makes precise about wave 78 §2.3.**  Its two arguments are for the *equality*
`B.r = 1`.  Only the `≥` half needs them; the `≤` half needs no Hilbert symbol and no field
theory, only the observation that the scalar `2` has even valuation at a ramified field.  The
whole difference between `ℚ₂(√±2)` (level `0`) and `ℚ₂(√±10)` (level `1`) is whether the field

## 3.  T-A2: the three congruences

### 3.1  The `D̄` congruence is free at `p = 0` (wave 79 §3's reduction, taken)

Wave 79 §3 identified this reduction and did not take it, reporting that it needs "the image of a
continuous marking is the closed subgroup generated by its values at the marked generators",
absent from the tree.  **That lemma is not needed.**  Run the argument on the preimage instead:

> if `H` is a **closed** subgroup of `Multiplicative ℤ₂` containing `ν'(dmGen α h i)` for every
> `i`, then `ν'⁻¹(H)` is a closed subgroup containing `Set.range (dmGen α h)`, hence contains the
> topological closure of the subgroup they generate, which is `⊤` by `MarkedCore.dm_topGen`.

That is `mpcRow_mem_of_gens`, four lines, standard three axioms.  At `H = 2ℤ₂`
(`mpcRow_evenSub`, closed because doubling is a continuous map out of the compact `ℤ₂`) it reads:
**a surjective marking is odd at one of the four marked generators** (`mpcRow_exists_odd_gen`).
At an `.Mpc α 1 ε η 0` row three of the four are already even:

* `ν'(C̄₀)`, because `χ_M(C̄₀) = 1` and the level is `1` (`MpcChiKerLevel`);
* `ν'(Ā) = −2^{α−1}·ν'(C̄₀)`, the frame's own relation (`nu_dmA_eq`);
* `ν'(B̄)`, exactly on the `ε = false` row, where that *is* the `B̄` clause.

Hence `mpcRow_dmD_odd_of_dmB_even`: at level one, surjective marking, `B̄` even implies `D̄` odd.
On the `ε = true` row the argument gives nothing, and that asymmetry is real: there `ν'(B̄)` is
odd, so surjectivity is already accounted for and `ν'(D̄)` is unconstrained by it.

The surjectivity input is not an assumption at these fields (§3 of the module):
`MarkedRecip.surjective_nu_ur_of_uniformizer` at `π = √a`, then `surjective_toAbK` and
`maxProPMk_surjective` down to `G_K(2)`, then the equivalence.

### 3.2  Three conjuncts are equivalence-free

Wave 79 had to assert all four conjuncts at `fieldId_chosenEquivM_sqrt{Ten,NegTen}`, which is a
`Classical` choice about which nothing is known beyond orientation.  Three of them do not need
it:

| conjunct | equivalence-free form | reading |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `B.r = 1` | `MpcNoUnitKerChi B` (with §2's `B.r ≤ 1`) | no `ker χ` class has unimodular `ν` |
| `ν'(B̄) ≡ p (mod 2)` | `MpcLambdaOne B (−1) p` | `λ(−1) = p` |
| `ν'(D̄) ≡ 1 (mod 2)` | `MpcLambdaOne B (mUnit 2) 1` | `λ(5) = 1` |
| `ν'(C̄₀) = γ·2` | **none** | see §3.3 |

`MpcLambdaOne B u c` says every class of cyclotomic value `u` has `ν_ur`-value `≡ c (mod 2)`.
This is the marked pair's `λ` at level one with no marked pair built (`MarkedRecip.nuMod` is the
same map), and it is well-posed for the same reason `λ` is: two classes with equal `χ` differ by
a `ker χ` class, whose `ν` is `2`-divisible at level `1`.  `mpcRow_congr_of_lambda` transports a
`λ`-value to *any* marked generator with that cyclotomic value, through *any* oriented
equivalence, which is what makes the restatement work.

It is also the selector's own vocabulary: `FieldBranchSelector.Matches` at an `.Mpc β r ε η` row
asks for `negOneVal = ε·2^{r−1} (mod 2^r)`, and `MpcLambdaOne B (−1) (p ε 1)` *is* that.

### 3.3  The pivot: a correction to wave 79 §2.1

Wave 79 §2.1 pairs the three congruences with the selector's three `Matches` conditions and reads
the pivot as `r = Q.r`.  **That pairing is not right at the `ε = true` row.**  The level gives
only `v₂(ν'(C̄₀)) ≥ B.r`; the pivot is the equality.  Since

    ν'(ker χ_M) = ⟨ν'(Ā), ν'(C̄₀), 2·ν'(B̄)⟩ = ⟨ν'(C̄₀), 2·ν'(B̄)⟩   (α = 2, nu_dmA_eq)

the exact level `= 2ℤ₂` says `min(v₂(ν'(C̄₀)), 1 + v₂(ν'(B̄))) = 1`, so:

* at `ε = false` (`ν'(B̄)` even) the second term is `≥ 2` and the pivot **follows**;
* at `ε = true` (`ν'(B̄)` odd) the second term is already `1` and the pivot **does not follow**.

A marking with `(ν'(Ā), ν'(B̄), ν'(C̄₀), ν'(D̄)) = (−8, 1, 4, 1)` satisfies the frame relation, has
exact level `1`, is surjective, and has both `λ`-values right for the `ε = true` row, while
failing the pivot.  (Hand check; the realizability of that marking on `D_M 2 0` is not
formalized.)  So the pivot is a genuinely independent fourth condition at `ℚ₂(√−10)`, and at
`ℚ₂(√10)` it is a consequence of the *lower* half of the level, `nu_ker_chi_ge`.

**Why even the `ε = false` case is not landed.**  The displayed identity for `ν'(ker χ_M)` is not
a theorem of this tree: it needs `ker χ_M ⊆ D_M` described well enough to compute its `ν`-image.
The honest form of that computation is the graph subgroup of `Multiplicative ℤ₂ × ℤ₂ˣ` cut out by
`λ`, and building it needs the **structure** of `ℤ₂ˣ` as `⟨−1⟩ × 5^{ℤ₂}`, not merely its
generation.  The tree has generation (`imChiM 2 = ⊤`, and `fieldId_hrange_sqrtTen` proves the
range is all of it); it does not have freeness.  That is the single missing input, and it is a
statement about `ℤ₂ˣ` alone, so it is cleanly ticketable.

All four inputs are values of the norm-group character.  For `K = ℚ₂(√d)`, `N(Kˣ) = ker (·,d)₂`,
and the level and the two `λ`-values read off it:

| input | says | at `d = 10` | at `d = −10` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `MpcNoUnitKerChi` | `2` is not a norm | `(2, 10)₂ = −1` ✓ | `(2, −10)₂ = −1` ✓ |
| `λ(−1) = p` | `−2` is a norm iff `p = 1` | `(−2, 10)₂ = −1`, so `p = 0` ✓ | `(−2, −10)₂ = +1` and `(−1, −10)₂ = −1`, so `p = 1` ✓ |

**All four values agree with the frozen rows** `Main.lean:136-137` (`p false 1 = 0`,

the campaign had performed.  These are hand computations with the standard dyadic formula

### 4.2  The descent that any of them needs, worked out

Even given the symbol, getting from "`2` is not a norm" to `MpcNoUnitKerChi` is a real argument,
because `MpcNoUnitKerChi` quantifies over `G_K^{ab}` and only `rec_K(Kˣ)` is under control.  The
chain, in the order it has to be built:

1. **`{g | IsUnit (ν_ur g)}` is open.**  `2ℤ₂` is closed (this lane's `mpcRow_isClosed_evenSub`),
   so its complement is open, and `ν_ur` is continuous.
2. **`ker χ` is only closed**, so density alone does not preserve it.  What survives is
   `χ(rec x) ≡ 1 (mod 8)`, an open condition; and `u ≡ 1 (mod 8)` implies `u` is a square in
   `ℤ₂ˣ`, hence a norm.  So the class group statement one lands on is still "`2^m` is a norm for
   some odd `m`", which is what the symbol refutes.
3. **`ν_ur(rec x)` must be turned into `v_K(x)`**, i.e. every `x : Kˣ` is a spectral unit times a
   power of `√a`.  This is the discreteness of the value group at `ℚ₂(√a)`; `Fields.lean` §4 has
   the separation lemma it needs (`norm_add_mul_sqrtIn`) but not the statement.
4. **`N` of a spectral unit is a `ℤ₂`-unit**, and at `ℚ₂(√10)` its residue mod `8` is `±1`
   (`x² − 10y² ≡ 1, 7 (mod 8)` for `x` a unit, `y ∈ ℤ₂`).  `Fields.normUnits_le_depthUnits`
   already proves the shape `u = x + y√a` with `‖x‖ = 1`, `‖y√a‖ < 1` that this needs.

Step 4 is the whole arithmetic; steps 1 to 3 are the descent.  Note that step 4 in this form
**replaces** the Hilbert symbol: the mod-`8` congruence on norms of units, plus `N(√10) = −10`,
determines the norm group directly, so the lane that closes this does not need a symbol
calculator at all.  That is the recommended shape of the next ticket.
