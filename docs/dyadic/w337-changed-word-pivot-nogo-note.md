# W337: changed words do not bypass M-pivot placement

W337 corrects the interpretation of W334.  Its changed-word data structures are valid
conditional transport interfaces, but constructing one is not easier than solving the
canonical pivot-placement problem.

For compact M and displayed Mpc, compose the adapted presentation's exact core map with
the inverse tame-compatible candidate-group equivalence.  This gives an exact compatible
boundary map for the corresponding standard word, hence a corrected standard-word
certificate on the intrinsic field core.  Transporting that core through any oriented M
equivalence produces a realized standard certificate on `DM`.  Characteristic orientation
then forces the distinguished C pivot.

The two forcing theorems and the explicit compact-M nonexistence corollary have the
standard trust base and are uniform in the depth and handle count.

Consequently the general campaign cannot evade the field-placement theorem by moving the
word, even though W335 and W336 provide correct general Nielsen and presentation-transport
machinery.  The genuine remaining even-M problem is the core automorphism/orbit theorem
placing the intrinsic mobile pivot on C (or an equivalent theorem that changes the
characteristic analytic model itself, not merely its presentation word).
