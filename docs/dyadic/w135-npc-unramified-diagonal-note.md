# W135: the honest unramified Npc diagonal

The one-variable diagonal left by the full-offset Npc Hessian is now explicit and quadratic.

- It is the sum of the two relevant factor-set graph diagonals and the quadratic value at the
  honest normal-coordinate offset.
- The two graph polarizations have equal factor-set errors; the normal cocycle equation cancels
  them, so quadraticity follows from the original quadratic form alone.
- Composing with the inverse normal-coordinate equivalence gives exactly the head quadratic
  required by the Npc phase model and its standard Gauss theorem.

The tempting identification with `npcQ0` composed with the inverse cross operator is generally
false and is not used.  All eight exported declarations use exactly the standard three axioms.
