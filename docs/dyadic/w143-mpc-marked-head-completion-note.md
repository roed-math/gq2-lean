# W143: M6 head completion reduced to the initial relator

The final uniform marked-source theorem is now equivalent to five explicit tower-cost families.

- M-frame approximation decomposes the three unrestricted tail slots into marked parts and
  B/C/D tower residues.
- Two tower costs vanish formally: the D tower in `um` and the C tower in `un`.
- Exactly five remain: C and D in `ut`, B and C in `um`, and D in `un`.
- Head completion is equivalent, for every permitted base pair, to absorption of those five
  families by the fully marked cost range.

Exact finite quotients verify the equality in depths 2, 3, and 4, but the all-degree initial
relator absorption theorem remains open and is not assumed.  Every exported declaration uses
exactly the standard three axioms.
