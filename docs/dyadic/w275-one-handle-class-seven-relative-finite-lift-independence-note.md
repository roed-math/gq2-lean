# W275 — class-seven finite lift independence

W274 showed that the ordinary class-seven relative obstruction is independent of the direct
relative lifts once their compact factors agree exactly.  W275 strengthens that statement to
the finite data actually visible at this rung: equality of the four compact factors modulo
`lambda_7` is enough.

The coordinatewise factor ratios now define ordinary relative depth-five classes.  Their four
standard coordinate shifts account exactly for the change in the direct residual.  The `C`
ratio is routed through the relaxed `C` row, because finite quotient equality does not imply the
extra character-neutrality enjoyed by exact relifts.

Therefore the relaxed target-membership criterion, its quotient obstruction, and the literal
row equation depend only on the compact factor classes modulo `lambda_7`.  Equivalently, the
ordinary zero-or-one rank jump cannot change when representatives of the same finite compact
packet are chosen.

The neutral-`C` refinement remains separate.  The remaining ordinary class-seven question has
now descended to a finite compact correction packet rather than an inverse-limit lift choice.
