# W248: the four rows of the direct one-handle span

W248 decomposes the W247 finite shift range into the join of its four coordinate images.
The coordinate inclusions are independent, and every finite correction is their product, so
the full range equals `oneHandleDirectCoordinateSpan` exactly.

The generic even crossed-derivation formulas identify the four images explicitly.  For a
finite correction class `e`, the A row is the product of its square with its brackets against
the current A and B letters.  The B row brackets against A, the character-neutral C row
brackets against D, and the D row brackets against C.  Both handle corrections are absent.

Thus `OneHandleDirectCoordinateSpanSupply` is equivalent to the W247 finite range supply and
implies `MpcPivShiftH 2 1`.  The remaining work is no longer an opaque word equation: it is
membership of the current inverse relator in the join of these four literal finite rows.
