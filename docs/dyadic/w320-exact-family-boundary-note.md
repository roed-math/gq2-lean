# W320: exact corrected-RN boundary for the selected family

W320 packages the complete range-matching selected-family campaign as one theorem:

`HasSelectedRowResidualRN B FF S ↔ EvenCanonicalMarkingBoundary B S`.

Here `S` is the canonical family selection built from the cyclotomic range.  The
forward implication uses W319's residual-to-pivot theorems on the compact-M rows;
the reverse implication is the existing uniform family construction.

The boundary predicate is trivial on the already closed L, N0, Npc, and
epsilon-true Mpc rows.  Its only substantive values are the exact distinguished-C
pivot on M0 and epsilon-false Mpc.  Consequently the corrected-family campaign no
longer has a list of unrelated class-specific analytic gaps: it has one precise
even-M arithmetic boundary.

This also records the correct direction for subsequent work.  W319 rules out
reusing the unchanged compact-M presentation on the strict-handle alternative, so
the remaining proof must either establish this field pivot intrinsically or supply
a genuinely different mobile-pivot presentation.
