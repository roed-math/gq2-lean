# W227: two coordinate equations suffice

W226 identifies the residual neutrality cokernel as a quotient of the sixteen-element deep
handle digit plane. W227 chooses its two standard generators: one unit `U` digit and one unit
`V` digit.

Every digit pair is a product of powers of these two generators. Therefore the coordinate
interaction range is the whole plane exactly when it contains both generators. Expanding
range membership gives two literal finite `C = A * B * D` equations, one for each prescribed
digit pair.

A uniform supply of these two equations kills every target-specific neutrality obstruction.
Together with the already-separated ordinary-relative coordinate coverage, it implies
`MpcPivShiftH 2 1`. The remaining work is now the construction of these two generator
relations (and the ordinary-relative coverage), not an arbitrary sixteen-case search.
